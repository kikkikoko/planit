share real trips

Publish places you’ve been or genuinely recommend, with advice that helps someone else travel well.

be kind

No harassment, hate, threats or bullying — of other travellers, locals or anyone else.

keep it safe and clean

No sexual or violent content, nothing illegal, and no advice that could put someone in danger.

no spam

No advertising, affiliate dumps, repeated posts or fake accounts.
