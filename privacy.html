planit

privacy policy

last updated 10 October 2026

the short version

planit is a travel planner. We keep what you create so you can plan trips, and we share only what you choose to publish. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

what stays on your phone

Everything you make in planit is saved on your device: trips, boards, hearts, notes, comments, quiz answers, your display name, your profile photo and any photos you attach. If you never create an account, none of this leaves your device except in the specific cases listed below.

what we store when you create an account

Your email address and password. Passwords are stored as a secure hash by our database provider, Supabase; we can never see them.

A synced copy of your planit (the same items listed above, including photos you attach), so you can sign in on another device and pick up where you left off.

Your block list, so travellers you block stay blocked on every device.

what other people can see

Only trips you choose to publish. A published trip shows the name you travel under, the trip’s title and destination, its days and stops, and any notes and photos you include with those stops. Anyone using planit can see published trips. You can unpublish a trip at any time, and it disappears from the feed straight away.

Comments you write on other travellers’ trips are currently kept on your own device and are not shown to other people.

reports and feedback

If you report a trip, a traveller or a comment, we store the report (what was reported, the reason and any details you add) and, if you’re signed in, your account ID, so we can review it. If you send feedback from the app, we store the message and, if you add it, your name or email. Only the planit team can read reports and feedback.

importing a link

When you paste a link to save a place, the link and any caption text that comes with it are sent to our server and processed by Anthropic’s Claude AI to suggest the place’s name and location. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models. We don’t keep the link after the place card is made.

maps and photos

On iPhone, maps are shown with Apple Maps; on the web, map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, these services receive your device’s IP address and the area of the map you’re viewing. Tapping “maps” opens Google Maps or Apple Maps, whose own privacy policies then apply.

Many place photos are provided by Unsplash photographers and load from Unsplash’s servers, which receive your IP address.

who processes your data for us

Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions) — data stored on Supabase’s servers.

Anthropic (link import, as described above).

Apple (if you download planit from the App Store, under Apple’s own terms).

We don’t sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we don’t share it for advertising.

how long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting your account (settings → account → delete account) permanently removes your account, your synced planit, your published trips and your block list from our database straight away. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. Reports you filed are kept without your account ID so we can keep the community safe.

your rights

If you’re in the UK or EU you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. You can delete your data yourself in the app at any time, or contact us using the details below. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

We rely on the contract with you (to provide the app you signed up for) and our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (for reports and blocks).

children

planit isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

changes

If we change how planit uses data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app before significant changes take effect.

contact

Use “contact us” in the app’s settings — it reaches the planit team directly.
